# Ande Mafenuka — Wardrobe Stylist, Cape Town

**Where African fashion research meets the shoot.**

Ande Mafenuka, known as Samba, is a wardrobe stylist working between Cape Town and Johannesburg, South Africa. He styles editorial, campaign and music-video shoots for fashion publications, brands and cultural institutions, including The Market Theatre Foundation and Vogue. His credited work covers menswear tailoring, occasion dressing, athleisure, and archival and folk dress. He also works as a designer and creative researcher, and has taken modelling and direction credits on his own editorial projects.
